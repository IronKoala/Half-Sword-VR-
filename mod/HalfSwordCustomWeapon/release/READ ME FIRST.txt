Half Sword Custom Weapon (test build)
=====================================

Puts your own 3D weapon model into Half Sword. You supply the model; none ship with this download.

What you need
- Half Sword (Steam).
- UE4SS 3.0.1 installed in Half Sword (https://github.com/UE4SS-RE/RE-UE4SS). Its files go in
  Half Sword\HalfswordUE5\Binaries\Win64 and it makes a "ue4ss\Mods" folder there.

Install the mod
1. Close Half Sword.
2. Copy the "HalfSwordCustomWeapon" folder from this zip into
   Half Sword\HalfswordUE5\Binaries\Win64\ue4ss\Mods\
   (it holds dlls\main.dll, CustomWeapon.ini, enabled.txt).

Add a weapon
1. Get a weapon model as GLB/glTF or FBX (best: they keep their textures), OBJ, or a Skyrim Special Edition
   weapon mod (.nif with its textures folder: pick the .nif, or drop the whole mod folder).
   Swords, daggers, axes, maces, hammers, spears, double-ended weapons, shields and flails (pick Flail as
   the weapon type; staff, chain and head must be separate pieces or named meshes).
2. Start WeaponConverter.exe and press Browse (or drop the model on the window, or on the exe).
3. Type the real length in cm (downloaded models often have odd scales) and the weight in kg if you
   know it (leave it empty and it is estimated). Weapon type can stay on Detect.
4. Press Convert and look at the picture: handle on the left, blade or head on the right, and the
   coloured bar under it marks pommel, grip, guard and blade. If the blade is on the left, press
   "Blade is on the wrong end".
5. Under "In the game", give it a name and tick Free play and/or Progression (progression: the
   merchant sells it from the rank you pick, at your price or one by rank). Swords: pick a scabbard,
   one of the game's (best fit picks the shortest that covers the blade), your own model (any model
   file; it is fitted to the blade's length at Install, mouth at the wider end unless you tick
   "mouth is the narrow end"), or none (worn on the belt).
6. Optional, under "Damage": which edges cut, the head type, edge and point sharpness, rigidity and the
   balance point. Left empty they come from the model's shape (the summary under the picture shows
   what was found).
7. Press "Install into Half Sword". It copies the weapon into the mod folder (weapons\<name>\).
   Nothing is written there before you press Install. "Install all" does every converted weapon.

In the game
- Your weapons are NEW weapons: in free mode they are in the weapon list of the inventory screen,
  next to the game's own. No game weapon is changed.
- The game saves them like its own weapons. Each one is saved as a game weapon of the same kind with
  a special name, so if you remove this mod, saved converted weapons turn into that game weapon
  instead of disappearing, and come back when you reinstall.
- Problems: see HalfSwordCustomWeapon\CustomWeapon.log.

Remove: delete the HalfSwordCustomWeapon folder from ue4ss\Mods.
